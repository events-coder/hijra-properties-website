A few useful answers

Questions before you take the next step?

Every opportunity is different. Speak with us to understand the information available and what the next steps may involve.

What kind of opportunities does Hijra Properties focus on?

We focus on property, development and strategic investment opportunities connected to Makkah and Madinah.

Can international buyers explore property opportunities?

Eligibility and ownership rules depend on the property, location and applicable Saudi regulations. We recommend confirming your circumstances with qualified local legal and property professionals before proceeding.

Do you work with developers and landowners?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from developers, landowners and other potential partners interested in residential or commercial projects in the two cities.

Do you guarantee investment returns?

No. Property and development investments carry risk, and returns are never guaranteed. Each opportunity should be assessed independently with appropriate professional advice.

Can you advise on residency pathways?

We can help you explore general information and point you toward relevant pathways. Eligibility and applications are determined by the applicable authorities and qualified advisers.
